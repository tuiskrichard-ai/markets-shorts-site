Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Markets Shorts Uploader is a private application used solely by its owner to upload original videos to the owner's own YouTube channel.

Data accessed

The application requests only the youtube.upload permission for the owner's own Google account. It uploads videos and their titles and descriptions. It does not read, collect, store or share any data about other users or viewers.

Data storage

The OAuth access token is stored only as an encrypted secret in the owner's private automation environment and is used exclusively for uploading videos.

Third parties

No personal data is sold or shared with third parties. Use of YouTube is subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
